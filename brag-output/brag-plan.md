# Plansphere — launch video plan

**Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 30fps, 20.0s · **Tone:** cinematic, dark premium (Nocturne UI)

## Answers
- **What is it?** A platform where a college runs its whole fest (events, registrations, QR gate entry, analytics) and students carry one pass.
- **Who is it for?** Fest organisers and the volunteers at the gate; students on the other side.
- **What sets it apart?** One QR per participant covers entry, team check-in and meals, and a second scan is refused, never double counted.
- **Most impressive claim:** "Three days, 42 events, one pass." (Ignitia '26 seed copy)
- **Visual hook:** that line, landing word by word over a blurple glow, while the camera pushes in.
- **Real UI shown:** scanner viewfinder → "Checked in" result, the Overview events table, the KPI strip, the Gate live feed. All built from the app's Nocturne tokens and copy.
- **Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.

## Angle
A slow, confident camera move through the real product at night-mode weight. Each scene has one idea and one line. The music is a D-minor pulse that swells into the logo.
